1. Master Layout Architecture Transformation

Main Viewport & Sidebar Left sidebar navigation (Dashboard, Regional Status, Guidelines, Contacts) consumes screen width[cite: 1]. Remove sidebar entirely[cite: 1]. Convert into a full-width container (100% or 1440px/1600px centered) to maximize data visualization area[cite: 1].
Visual Scaffolding Scattered cards with ambiguous section dividers. Implement a Card-Based Architecture. Every module must feature clear borders, rounded corners, and soft navy/slate header accents to group content visually[cite: 1].
Filtering Behavior Manual filter trigger requiring "Apply Filter" and "Clear All Filters" button clicks[cite: 1]. Convert to a Reactive Auto-Filter toolbar[cite: 1]. Dropdown changes must update the dashboard state and trigger re-renders instantly without confirmation buttons[cite: 1].
Tooltip Interactions Requires clicking cards or legends to view descriptions. Switch to Instant Hover Tooltips across charts, choropleth maps, and metric badges[cite: 1].
Visual Hierarchy General hazards mixed with mental health data; regional scoring omitted. Strictly align sequence to Slides 1–8: Header → Overview KPIs → Geographic Distribution → Patient Classifications → Demographics → Suicide Module → Tables → Secondary Hazards → Regional Performance[cite: 1].

3. Detailed Functional & Section Specifications

Header Bar & Auto-Filter Toolbar

  • Insert the official Department of Mental Health logo directly adjacent to the primary headline[cite: 1].
  • Rename the dashboard title from "Dashboard" to "Social Listening 2569"[cite: 1].
  • Add the subheader: "กองบริหารระบบบริการสุขภาพจิต" (Mental Health Service System Administration Division)[cite: 1].
  • Display [Reset Chart Layout] and [Reload Data] action triggers alongside the last synchronization timestamp[cite: 1].
  • Strip out all "Apply Filter" and "Clear Filters" action buttons; rebind dropdown select inputs to mutate state reactively[cite: 1].
  • Remove all English secondary text in dropdown lists (e.g., retain Thai month abbreviations ม.ค. – ธ.ค. and strip out "Jan", "Feb", etc.)[cite: 1].
  • Standardize regional labels to "เขตสุขภาพที่ 1" through "เขตสุขภาพที่ 13" (replace truncated terms like "เขต 1")[cite: 1].

Section 1: Critical SLAs & 4 Risk Factors / 5 Warning Signs

  • Top KPI Row (SLA Cards): Display four primary metrics: Total Events (440), Black Tier (0 - 1 hr response), Red Tier (4 - 24 hr response), Yellow Tier (436 - 72 hr response) with hover descriptions[cite: 1].
  • 4 Key Risk Factors of Violent Patients:
    • Update header title to "4 ปัจจัยหลักของผู้ป่วยที่ก่อความรุนแรง" (strip the term "รายเก่า" / previous cases)[cite: 1].
    • Encase within a prominent, high-contrast container with an embedded summary banner: "จากเหตุการณ์ทั้งหมด XXX เหตุการณ์ มีปัจจัยเสี่ยง XXX เหตุการณ์ (XX.X%)"[cite: 1].
    • Display the 4 factors: 1. Missed appointment/medication default, 2. Relapsed drug use, 3. Concurrent substance abuse, 4. Others[cite: 1].
    • Calculation Rule: The denominator must solely aggregate diagnosed psychiatric patients (new/existing) and substance users (new/existing)[cite: 1]. Exclude the non-psychiatric / non-substance user group from the denominator[cite: 1].
  • 5 Early Warning Signs:
    • Remove the subheader phrase "เฝ้าระวังกลุ่มเสี่ยงในชุมชน"[cite: 1].
    • Embed a centered summary box: "จากเหตุการณ์ทั้งหมด XXX เหตุการณ์ มีสัญญาณเตือน XXX เหตุการณ์ (XX.X%)"[cite: 1].
    • Calculation Rule: Denominator must be based on total events (all individuals can exhibit symptoms)[cite: 1].
    • Render symptom items (irritability/rage, paranoia, insomnia, talking to self, pacing) with visual avatar icons[cite: 1].
    • Remove category percentage labels and add footnote: "หมายเหตุ: 1 เหตุการณ์สามารถพบสัญญาณเตือนมากกว่า 1 สัญญาณ"[cite: 1].

Section 2: Monthly Trends & Geospatial Distribution

  • Monthly Trends: Render an Area/Line Chart covering October 2025 to June 2026[cite: 1].
  • Thailand Choropleth Map: Standardize the five color thresholds: ≥ 61, 46 - 60, 31 - 45, 16 - 30, and 1 - 15 events[cite: 1]. Hovering over a province must trigger an instant tooltip with provincial metrics[cite: 1].
  • Top 10 Provinces: Display as a cleanly formatted horizontal bar chart sorted descending[cite: 1].

  2. New Psychiatric Patient (ผู้ป่วยจิตเวชรายใหม่)[cite: 1]

  3. Top 5 Suicide Causes: Horizontal bar chart showing leading causes[cite: 1].
  4. Top 5 Incident Locations: Reduce former Top 10 view down to the top 5 locations (Residence, River/Waterway, Dormitory/Condo, Public Area/Others, Vehicle)[cite: 1].
  5. Top 5 Suicide Methods: Bar chart identifying primary means[cite: 1].

Include an emergency support footer banner highlighting: Mental Health Hotline 1323 and Samaritans of Thailand 02-109-7995[cite: 1].
